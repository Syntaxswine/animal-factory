# Gun and ammunition death drops

Six original 1254 × 1254 RGBA PNG sprites generated with built-in image_gen. Files: `dist/assets/environment/loot/`. Reference: `dist/assets/characters/armed/pig-foreman-assault.png`. Generated alpha is preserved without image processing.

`dist/tactics/loot-art.html` previews individual files and actual drop rendering at 2× camera zoom. `loot-art.js` displays the dead unit's equipped firearm, plus matching ammunition if loaded rounds remain. These are visual ground drops, not collectible inventory. Knife and unarmed deaths keep the fallen marker. Drops persist with the map's units and obey explored tiles and the current floor. Actual ammo quantity is not represented by the cartridge count in the art.
