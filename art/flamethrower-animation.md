# Flamethrower fire

Preview: `dist/tactics/flame-art.html` (animal, uniform, stance, outcome, range, direction, zoom, speed, scrubbing and a filmstrip). Review sheet: `art/flamethrower-animation-review.png` (Red Hats goat burning down a foreman at four tiles). Code: `dist/tactics/flame-effect.js`. Nozzle table: `dist/tactics/flame-nozzles.js`, generated by `tools/measure-flame-nozzles.mjs`.

This is a code-drawn effect over the existing painted sprites, not generated imagery. Each flame shape is filled as one union in five flat bands (outline, red rim, orange body, ochre inner, cream core), so the dark outline traces only the outside edge, as it does on the sprites. The colours are sampled from the sprites: their outline `#2f2014`, the red neckerchiefs, brass fittings and cream highlights. Smoke is warm grey lobed clouds that grow, drift and shrink away; they never turn translucent, because the outline would show through.

It stays lighthearted: no burning bodies. An animal burned down vanishes in a round puff of soot while a few tufts of its coat drift down, a missed target hops in surprise, and the projector coughs out a smoke ring when the fuel stops.

## One burst (1.25 s)

| ms | On screen |
|---|---|
| 0–70 | Pilot flare at the nozzle. |
| 70–260 | The jet leaves along the barrel axis and bends onto the landing point. It is a smooth rod near the nozzle; rolling billows and upward tongues begin a fifth of the way out, where the fuel has slowed. |
| 260 | It lands: a flame blossom on the torso (hit), a soot poof over the whole body (burned down), or a splash on the floor beside the target, a little short, on a side chosen per shot (miss). |
| 640–830 | Fuel stops; the tail detaches from the nozzle and the last fuel lands. The smoke ring leaves the nozzle. |
| 830–1250 | Smoke rises, embers fall back, tufts drift down. |

The shooter shakes slightly while fuel flows. A survivor shivers. A burned-down target keeps its standing sprite until 50 ms after the flame lands, so the swap to the fallen marker happens inside the soot. Players with reduced motion enabled see one still frame for the burst and no shaking.

## Game integration

Attack events in `engine.js` now carry `shooter`, `target` and `downed` (the ids each impact put down, including neighbours of a tank blast). `app.js` plays an attack's events back to back as before: bullets keep 650 ms and flame events take 1.25 s (`effectClock`). Combat timing is otherwise unchanged. The game loop still does not wait for effects, so another attack later in the same guard turn replaces a burst still on screen.

## Nozzles

All 48 flamethrower sprites face right, with the projector as their rightmost part. The tool takes the rightmost column holding at least three opaque pixels as the muzzle face. It fits the barrel axis through the centre of the opaque run, followed up to 44 px leftward, skipping the flared muzzle and samples swollen by a hand or fitting. The table stores `[x, y, angle]` in sprite pixels. Negative angles raise the barrel, as on most standing poses. Re-run the tool after re-exporting flamethrower sprites: `npm run check` fails, naming the tool, when the table and the PNGs disagree. The pig director has no Red Hats uniform, so a Red Hats director falls back to the vector overlay's pilot-light position.

## Validation

- `tests/tactics-flame-effect.test.mjs`:
  - The table equals a fresh measurement of all 48 PNGs, and synthetic level and rising barrels measure correctly.
  - Placement repeats `app.js` for both facings and for the overlay.
  - The effect clock keeps the old 650 ms rule for bullet-only sequences.
  - Every number a burst draws is finite, and every filled subpath is wound the same way. This is checked for all outcomes, three barrel directions, far, near and zero-length landings, and still frames, at 10 ms steps.
  - Bursts are deterministic per seed. Pose windows and miss landings are checked too.
  - The suite catches a counter-wound jet rod (which cuts holes wherever billows overlap it), a stale table angle, and an out-of-range noise index (which dropped soot lobes as NaN geometry).
- `tests/tactics-flamethrower.test.mjs`: events name the shooter, the target and the units put down, for a burn, a miss, a tank blast and a graze.
- `tests/tactics-pages-files.test.mjs`: the Pages file list includes every local module and page its listed files reference.
- Browser: the real `app.js` was stepped frame by frame on a virtual clock, on a custom map with flamethrower guards, and photographed through a miss and a burn-down.
- The engine change adds information without changing outcomes. Running the balance bot's loop on seeds 1947–1966, the whole end state (hashed without `s.effect`) matched `efe6cfe` on 20 of 20 seeds, with 13 flamethrower bursts along the way.

## Open

- Burning units still show only `[FIRE n]`, and burning ground is a static flame. Both could reuse these flame and smoke shapes.
- Tank explosions still draw a flat orange ellipse.
- The enemy turn does not wait for a burst to finish.
