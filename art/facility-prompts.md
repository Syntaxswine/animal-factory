# Facility collection 01

Eight original sprites generated with built-in image_gen, based on docs/tactics/STORY.md: institutional laboratories, treatment rooms and detention facilities in the existing Soviet industrial art style. The botanical specimen is deliberately generic; the resource species and fictional process remain undecided.

Files: dist/assets/environment/facility/*.png. Preview: dist/tactics/facility-art.html. All originals preserve generated alpha. Props and shared-edge bars are art deliverables; placement anchors, world-scale calibration, traversal, cover and interaction rules remain integration work. Jail bars and the closed cell doorway are separate designs, not registered animation frames. Do not assume an open state or an operable door exists.

Style references: dist/assets/environment/workbench-metal.png for laboratory and medical props; dist/assets/environment/fence-railing.png for detention barriers.

## Final jail-bar revision

The first railing-based panel was discarded. The selected fixed bar panel is an image_gen edit of jail-door-closed.png, matching its tall frame and camera. Exact revision prompt:

Use case: precise-object-edit. Create a fixed jail-bar wall panel by modifying this exact barred cell door sprite. Preserve the exact outer rectangular frame, post heights and widths, source canvas 1254x1254, camera, scale, framing, baseline, lighting and weathered material. Do not shorten or widen the frame or change its proportions. Remove only the two right-side hinges and the left-side lock box and keyhole. Remove the inner hinged door-leaf frame; replace it with seven evenly spaced slim fixed vertical bars attached directly to the top and bottom outer frame rails. Add one slim horizontal reinforcing bar across the middle, parallel to the top rail in the fence plane. This must read as a tall floor-to-ceiling cell wall, matching the original door height, not a low outdoor railing. No lock, no door mechanism. Genuine alpha transparent gaps between bars and background, no ground or new backdrop. Retain all other visual traits of the source.
